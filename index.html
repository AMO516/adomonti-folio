about.

I am the designer you bring in when you need someone who can hold the big picture and sweat the details

Ciao, I’m Adolfo. An Italian designer specializing in brand, UX/UI and web design.

I started in 2006 and worked as typeface designer, brand designer, editorial, web designer, product designer, and headed the design department. Along the way I learned that good design is not just how it looks, but how it converts, retains, and scales.

Today I help founders build brands and websites that are clear and consistent, and mobile interfaces that people actually want to use.

Away from the screen, I do lettering, and I’m building my first videogame.
