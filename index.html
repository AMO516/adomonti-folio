about.

A designer for projects that need a view of the big picture and an eye for detail

Ciao, I’m Adolfo. An Italian designer specializing in brand and web design.

With 20+ years of experience, from junior all the way up to product designer and design department lead. I have worked on many kinds of projects: typefaces, logos and identities, books, websites, packaging, and mobile and dashboard interfaces. Good design is not only how it looks, but how it works, delivering your message to your customers.

Today I use that experience to help founders and businesses find their voice. I design brands and websites that are clear and consistent, and mobile interfaces that people actually want to use.
